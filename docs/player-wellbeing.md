# Player wellbeing

From British Esports tips, NHS psychiatry input to Gaming the Mind, and Chichester sleep research.

- Sleep 7–9 hours (teens often more). All-nighters wreck aim and mood. Fixed wake time; no ranked in bed; caffeine cut-off afternoon.
- Breaks, 20-20-20 eyes, 30 minutes movement most days.
- Eat away from the desk; water over a fourth energy drink.
- Burnout = exhaustion + cynicism + "I hate the game." Rest days are training.
- Keep one identity outside rank.
- If you cannot cut down despite harm to sleep, college or relationships, see a GP or the National Centre for Gaming Disorders.
