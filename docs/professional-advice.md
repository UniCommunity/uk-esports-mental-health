# Advice from UK clinicians and performance staff

1. Sleep first.
2. Treat anxiety/depression with standard NHS therapies; the game is context unless ICD-11 gaming disorder criteria are met.
3. No antidepressants alone in under-18s (NICE NG134).
4. Safety plan if there are suicidal thoughts.
5. Cut late caffeine and alcohol before adding supplements.
6. Team psychologists help burnout; they do not replace 111/999.
7. Confiscating the PC without a plan often explodes conflict.
8. Collapsing function → NCGD or CAMHS, not a motivational speech.

Plan and intent to die → 999.
