# Crisis and helplines

**Danger now:** 999 or A&E.

**Urgent mental health:** NHS 111 (mental health option). Scotland NHS 24 111.

- Samaritans **116 123**
- Text **SHOUT** to **85258**
- PAPYRUS **0800 068 4141** (under 35)
- Childline **0800 1111**
- Scotland Breathing Space 0800 83 85 87
- Wales CALL 0800 132 737
- Northern Ireland Lifeline 0808 808 8000

**NHS National Centre for Gaming Disorders** treats people 13+ whose gaming is out of control and harming life. Self-referral accepted. Not for "I play a lot and I am fine."

If a teammate says they want to die: take it seriously, stop the match, stay with them, call 111 or 999. Captains should keep numbers in the team doc.
