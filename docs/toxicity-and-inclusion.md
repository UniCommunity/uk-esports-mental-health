# Toxicity, harassment and inclusion

Harassment is a mental health exposure. Nottingham/NTU research found very high gender-related harassment among women and TGD competitive gamers, who then avoid voice chat and lose performance.

Players: mute, report, leave voice. Threats that mention finding you offline are a safeguarding issue (111/999/platform/campus).

Orgs and publishers: ping systems, role queue, sanctions, moderated official Discords, no forced voice. Expert Delphi work on esports mental health guidelines agreed environments must be free from harassment and that publishers and tournament organisers share responsibility.
