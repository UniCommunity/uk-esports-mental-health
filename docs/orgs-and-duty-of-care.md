# Teams, organisations and events

Duty of care applies to university societies and LANs, not only contracted rosters.

1. Crisis numbers in the handbook and on overlays.
2. Named welfare lead who is not the shot-caller.
3. If a player discloses suicidal thoughts: stop the scrim, stay, 111/999, safeguarding lead if under 18.
4. Sleep and travel plans for LANs.
5. A route to a psychologist or NHS talking therapies.
6. Anti-harassment code for talent and chat.

Coaches: Mental Health First Aid. "Mental toughness" is not a reason to ignore insomnia or panic.
