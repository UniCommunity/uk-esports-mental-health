# NHS, NICE, NIHR and WHO

Most UK young people who play games do not have a disorder.

ICD-11 **gaming disorder**: impaired control; increasing priority; continuation despite harm; significant functional impairment; typically 12 months.

Hours are not the diagnosis.

## NHS

GP, CAMHS and talking therapies for anxiety/depression. **National Centre for Gaming Disorders** (13+) for specialist care. NICE NG134, NG223, NG225 still apply. YoungMinds tells parents it is legitimate to raise gaming with a GP.

Manchester #BeeWell is a major NIHR-adjacent school dataset that includes gaming. Esports-specific NIHR trials are still fewer than school CBT trials.

## WHO

ICD-11 gaming disorder; WHO–ITU safe listening for game audio; concern about junk-food marketing on esports streams.

Skin betting sits next to NHS gambling treatment — some people need both services.
