# Formal and informal education

NICE NG223 applies to Rocket League clubs as much as rugby. RSHE can cover sleep, online conduct and when gaming stops being fun. British Esports school materials already list breaks and Childnet — pair with a pastoral path, not a shame assembly.

Informal educators are NUEL/NSE captains and Discord mods. Give them 111 and 116 123.

UK esports scholarships (e.g. Roehampton) should include welfare check-ins. Academic progress is part of duty of care if the university pays people to compete.

Parents: YoungMinds — worry when the young person is exhausted, isolated, or cannot cut down. Start with the GP.
