# Gambling, skins, stimulants

Skin betting and loot boxes sit next to esports. If money or skins chase losses: GamCare / National Gambling Helpline **0808 8020 133** and the GP. NHS gambling treatment is separate from the National Centre for Gaming Disorders.

Energy drinks and stimulant misuse wreck sleep. Hearing: WHO–ITU safe listening; tournament peaks can be dangerous.
