# Suicide prevention and bereavement

Use UK national strategies. Esports extras: do not clip a dead player’s last stream; mods should strip method detail and add Samaritans/PAPYRUS; teams need a postvention plan (who speaks, who supports the roster, when to pause fixtures).

Bereavement: Help is at Hand, SOBS 0300 111 5065, Facing the Future, PAPYRUS.

Asking a teammate if they are thinking about suicide does not plant the idea.

Fuller UK map: https://github.com/UniCommunity/uk-mental-health-awareness
