# UK Esports Mental Health Hub

Mental health information for **players, teams, coaches, university societies, organisers, parents and community clubs** in the UK esports sector.

Competitive play can build skill, friendship and career paths. It can also collide with sleep loss, burnout, online abuse, gambling-like mechanics and a culture that treats "the grind" as unpaid overtime. This hub treats those as design and duty-of-care problems, not personal weakness.

> **If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, get help now.**

| Service | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Emergency | **999** |
| NHS urgent mental health | **111** (mental health option) · [111.nhs.uk](https://111.nhs.uk) |
| Samaritans | **116 123** (24/7) |
| Shout | Text **SHOUT** to **85258** |
| PAPYRUS HOPELINE247 (under 35) | **0800 068 4141** |
| Childline (19 and under) | **0800 1111** |
| NHS National Centre for Gaming Disorders (13+) | Search "NHS National Centre for Gaming Disorders" — self-referral accepted |

This repo is **education, not treatment**.
